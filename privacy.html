Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Hermes Sheets, a private desktop integration operated by the Google account owner.

Google data accessed

The application may access Google Drive files and Google Sheets spreadsheets that the owner is authorized to use.

How data is used

Data is used only to complete actions requested by the owner, such as reading, reviewing, or editing a spreadsheet. Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or model training.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and working evidence are stored locally on the owner's device. Credentials are not committed to public source control. Access is limited to the scopes shown on the Google consent screen.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties except when required to complete an owner-requested action through Google APIs.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls local files and can delete credentials at any time. Google access can also be revoked from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through the repository issue tracker.
